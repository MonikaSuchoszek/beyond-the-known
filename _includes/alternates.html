{%- comment -%} Works out which locales actually have a counterpart for this page, matched on post_id for posts and pages, otherwise falling back to the locale's index. Sets `available_list`: an array of "~" entries, in site.languages order. Renders nothing itself. Used by header.html for the language switcher and by head.html for the rel="alternate" hreflang annotations, so that the two can never disagree about what the translations of a page are. URLs come out in canonical form, since that is what hreflang has to point at and the switcher may as well link straight there too. The list itself is computed once per build by _plugins/precompute_navigation.rb and stored as `page.alternates`; looping over site.posts and site.pages here on every page made the build ~5x slower (see speedup.md). {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign available_list = page.alternates -%}
